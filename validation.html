03 / From research to application

Explore. Test. Validate. Apply.

Not every experiment becomes a product — and that is the point. Here is how an idea earns its way to the pitch, in three steps.

01

How to read it

Every idea runs the same eight hurdles, in order. Only what clears all eight reaches players and coaches.

  1. 01Research questionIs it a real football question someone needs answered?
  2. 02Benchmark & testDoes it work on clips we already understand?
  3. 03Real-world trialDoes it hold up in a real session, with real players?
  4. 04Review & challengeCan specialists and partners break it?
  5. 05Bias & robustnessDoes it work for every age, pitch and light?
  6. 06Human oversightCan a person check it and overrule it?
  7. 07Deployment decisionShould it be used at all, and how?
  8. 08Learning loopWhat it taught us goes into the next question.
