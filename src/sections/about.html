06 / People & Standards

Innovation with
responsibility.

Our principles are not a policy page. Some lines move with training. These six never do.

01

Lines we never cross

Everything else on this site moves. These do not. Pick a line to see where it has already stopped something.

  1. 01

    Data minimised

    Only the data a defined question needs.

    In practiceA clip becomes 17 key points per player. Faces, voices and names are not part of the question.
  2. 02

    Purpose stated

    What the system measures, and what it must never be used to infer.

    In practiceTalent prediction from under-10 footage was asked for — and stopped at the question.
  3. 03

    Uncertainty shown

    Every number carries its error and confidence — never “exact”.

    In practiceBall found in 88% of daylight frames, and in 4% at night. Both numbers are published.
  4. 04

    Minors protected

    Consent on file for every young player; the system keeps an anonymous ID, never a name, age or school.

    In practiceIn the data, a young player is “ID 07”. Who they are never enters the system.
  5. 05

    Human decides

    Outputs go to a coach for review — never straight to a child or parent.

    In practiceThe model marks 8 of 20 frames as low confidence. A coach decides what they mean.
  6. 06

    Limits documented

    Known failure modes are stated next to the result.

    In practiceLow light, small and distant players: listed beside every result, not in a footnote.
